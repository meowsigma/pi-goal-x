# Technical design

Maintain session-scoped no-progress state in `registerGoalEvents`:

- `consecutiveNoProgressTurns`
- `noProgressRecoveryAttempt`

At `agent_end`, after existing cancellation/error checks:

1. If `core.goalWorkToolCalledThisTurn` is true, reset both fields and schedule the ordinary post-settlement continuation.
2. Otherwise increment the counter.
3. For attempts 1 and 2, arm a bounded recovery prompt and schedule one post-settlement continuation.
4. At attempt 3, do not schedule a continuation and emit one warning notification.

At `before_agent_start`, a user-driven turn resets the breaker. A matching checkpoint consumes and appends any armed recovery prompt to the authoritative active-goal system fragment.

The recovery prompt is generated by a pure bounded helper so wording and size are directly testable. Existing lifecycle-generation and actionable-goal checks remain authoritative.
