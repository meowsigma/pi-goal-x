## Requirements

1. **One continuation owner.** Goal-x owns focused-goal continuation. NQA contributes question/authorization guidance only; it must not force synthetic activity, retry stalled execution, or start a competing standalone recovery loop.
2. **Justified work.** A progress review recommending work supplies a bounded next action, expected observation/outcome, and how it changes a decision or verifies a criterion. Include the previous recommendation and bounded recent tool/outcome evidence; a generic restatement of the objective is insufficient. Activity alone must not erase retained strategic advice. Check progress periodically even during busy tool cycles so status-file edits cannot indefinitely postpone review; this is review admission, not an arbitrary stopping rule.
3. **Quiet incomplete state.** A structured independent review may report no justified next action. Persist that as a continuation hold on the ACTIVE, incomplete goal, never a fabricated pause/completion. No timer/checkpoint repeatedly retries an unchanged hold. A genuine user instruction, changed goal scope/configuration, or provenance-bearing completion of already-owned work can re-admit appropriate review/work. Ordinary status queries preserve a hold or scheduled wait. Preserve genuine future dependency wakes and their deadlines.
4. **Bounded failed recovery.** Ignored strategic advice and exhausted reviewer infrastructure admission end in quiet incomplete state, not repeated arbitrary work or endless ten-minute recovery timers. Retain diagnostics. No failure is approval; independent final audit, task/child contracts, budgets, and cancellation remain intact.
5. **Current lifecycle truth.** Immediately before provider use, inject a bounded current goal identity/status/continuation snapshot into model-visible context. Historical assistant prose, summaries, checkpoint text, and external reminders cannot establish current lifecycle. Detect an unproductive false-paused response for an active focused goal and give one precise reconciliation/review path, not an endless replay. Genuine paused/unfocused/budget-limited goals must never be resumed by this repair.
6. **Astra routing.** User defaults and diagnostic/reviewer/oracle routing become openai-codex/gpt-6-astra with high effort. Ordinary bounded workers remain Luna. Replace retired ox-alpha default/diagnostic routes without silent fallback. Existing sessions retain their explicit selections until human reload/new-session actions; do not impersonate those actions.
7. **Diagnostic instruction hygiene.** No unconditional Ponytail injection in diagnostic sessions; keep its skill available explicitly for implementation. Full-throttle/Ultra/Superpowers implementation ceremonies are opt-in for diagnosis rather than mandatory. Preserve testing/security/authorization requirements. Scope the local Qwen summarization payload modifier to the explicitly configured local Qwen route, not Astra/other providers.
8. **Terminal retry admission.** Explicit authentication/permission/credit-exhaustion/retired-model errors do not automatically retry or emit a goal checkpoint storm. As subsequently clarified by the user, preserve indefinite retry only for positively identified traffic/connectivity failures such as recoverable 400, 429, 5xx and WebSocket/network errors. Cap other hidden reissues at three, retain stricter empty-output nudging, and allow genuine user-requested retry after conditions change. Terminal evidence overrides transient wrappers. Goal must not run a competing secondary retry while pi-retry owns transport recovery.
9. **Honest observability.** Persist bounded progress-review receipts (route/selected effort, decision, evidence, timing, usage/error) locally. Record loaded goal/NQA code identity at initialization and request-time lifecycle/model/effort plus prompt hashes/appropriately redacted snapshots. Label hook-stage payload capture as such if later hooks could modify it; never claim server model identity from client labels. Avoid credentials, request headers, full tool output, and unbounded log growth.

## Acceptance clarifications after independent review

- An unavailable progress reviewer enters an honest durable hold on the **first** failure; no invisible retry owner is implied. Re-admission requires new input, a relevant configuration/scope change, or a fresh owned terminal receipt.
- Status questions, negated lifecycle instructions, correct “not paused” statements, and old/foreign terminal notifications cannot clear holds or replace future wakes. Owned work must have a successful goal/session-scoped launch receipt; a terminal notice is only permission to inspect its outcome, not evidence that a criterion passed.
- Refresh lifecycle at every context request, including within a multi-tool run. This latest frame supersedes older turn-start frames and never authorizes work for paused, unfocused, or budget-limited goals.
- Diagnostic retention is a small best-effort local ring, separate from the authoritative lifecycle ledger. It contains selected route/effort, hashes and secret-redacted bounded summaries, never headers or full payloads. Initialization hashes describe source snapshots; hook-stage capture is not final-wire or served-model attestation.
